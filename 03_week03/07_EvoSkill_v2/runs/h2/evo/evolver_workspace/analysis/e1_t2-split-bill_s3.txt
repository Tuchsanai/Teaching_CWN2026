**How it works**
- The amount to split is `total × (100 + tip_percent) / 100`, computed exactly with `Fraction`. It is rounded half-up to whole satang once, at the end.
- The result is split evenly with `divmod`. The first `remainder` people each pay one extra satang.

- Valid ranges: `total >= 0`, `people >= 1`, `0 <= tip_percent <= 100`.
