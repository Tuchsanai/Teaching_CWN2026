I followed the `./skills` guidance (`satang-money-math`, `thai-spec-reading`, `solution-verification`): integer satang, one rounding step at the end, and a self-check before reporting.

**Rules as implemented**
- `amount` must be an int ≥ 0 (in satang). Otherwise it raises `ValueError`.
- `tier` is matched case-insensitively. Anything other than silver/gold/platinum raises `ValueError`.
- Below 10,000 satang (100 baht) the result is 0. Exactly 100 baht qualifies.
- Raw cashback = amount × rate (1.5% / 2.5% / 4%).
- If `birthday` is true, the raw amount is doubled.
- The result is rounded half-up to a whole satang, then capped at 20,000 satang (200 baht) per bill.
